# **BOGGLE**## AS PLAYED**SECTION I - WORD FORMATION**1. Words must be at least 3 letters in length2. Letters must be sequentially adjoining3. Any given letter may only be used once in a word**SECTION II - SCORING**1. After filtering discovered words to ensure they are words permissible for scoring2. Most any word will do, following these rules:   1. The word can be located within either the Oxford English Dictionary, Merrium-Webster's Dictionary, or the Collin's Dictionary   2. It is a noun, adjective, verb, adverb, gerund, participle, articles, possessive article, quantifying article, demonstrative article, written numbers1. Word types not permissible: Conjunctions, Abbreviations, Morphemes, Pronouns
