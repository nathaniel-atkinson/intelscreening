# **CASTING SHADOWS**

## NON-COMPULSORY RULE PACK



SECTION I - VARIANT RULES

1. **ACTION POINTS**

   1. Unused action points may be preserved from turn to turn--only a total of 3 such reserved action points may be held at any given time, making an action cap of 7.
2. **RESOURCE TOKENS**

   1. Unspent resource tokens may be preserved from turn to turn--five tokens only may be reserved in this way at any given time
3. **COMPANION CARDS**

   1. Players may discard their companion to select a new one, provided there are companions cards left on the table to select from, costing an action point--discarded companions are to be removed out of play for the remainder of the game
